Dear Friends of EFP: Children's Stories,

On Tuesday, December 1, people around the world will come together for Giving Tuesday, a global day of generosity. This year, the Enduring Families Project: Children's Stories is taking part, and we hope you'll join us.

Our goal is to raise $10,000 to print and share our second book, “Lifting As We Rise: African American Women's Movement in the 19th Century”, along with its classroom materials, with schools and libraries across the Coulee Region and beyond.

I'm Rebecca Mormann-Krieger, historian and co-founder of the project, and a longtime classroom teacher. Since 2018, we have recovered the stories of the La Crosse area's early African American residents and brought them back to our children through books, lessons and performed monologues. Many teachers in our area already use them.
