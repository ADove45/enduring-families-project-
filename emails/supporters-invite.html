A story that was almost lost

In 1859, Arkansas passed a law forcing free Black people to leave the state or be enslaved. One young mother fled north with her two-year-old son. That boy, George Edwin Taylor, was raised on a farm near West Salem, and in 1904 he became the first African American to run for President of the United States.

Most people have never heard his name. Our book A Long Way to Find a Home is changing that. EFP has given 1,000 storybooks and 6,000 coloring books free to schools, libraries and community centers.
Children of color need to see people who look like them in the history of the place they live. All children need to know that their community's story is larger than a single story.

What's next

Our second book, Lifting As We Rise: African American Women's Movement in the 19th Century, is written and ready for printing. This Giving Tuesday, December 1, we're raising $10,000 to print it and share it, with its classroom materials, across our community.
