Hello,

Thank you for joining the Enduring Families Project: Children's Stories. We're so glad you're here.

Since 2018, Enduring Families Project: Children's Stories has worked to recover the stories of the La Crosse area's early African American residents. We began with actors performing monologues in libraries, museums and schools. Today our work includes videos, classroom lessons and, since 2025, children's books about real people from our region's past.
Children of color need to see people who look like them in the history of the place they live. All children need to know that their community's story is larger than a single story.

Our stories

"A Long Way to Find a Home" tells the childhood of George Edwin Taylor, who grew up in West Salem and in 1904 became the first African American to run for President of the United States. EFP has printed 1,000 storybooks and 6,000 coloring books, free to schools, libraries and community centers.

Our second book, "Lifting As We Rise: African American Women's Movement in the 19th Century", is written and ready for printing.

What you can expect from us
