Did you know?

Before he ran for president, George Edwin Taylor owned a newspaper in La Crosse.

Taylor grew up near La Crosse. By 1886 he owned and edited his own weekly paper, the Wisconsin Labor Advocate. A Black business owner was rare in 1880s La Crosse. In 1904 he became the first Black man to run for President of the United States. Original copies of his paper are still kept at the La Crosse Public Library Archives. Watch his monologue

Sources: La Crosse Public Library Archives; UW–La Crosse Murphy Library
